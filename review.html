FIRST / EXPLORE BOTH DIRECTIONS

Spend a few minutes in each. Find a relevant service, a market and one project, then try the demo enquiry. Which journey would work best for a regional marketing or event owner?

  1. Can you understand what NOW does without instructions?
  2. Can you find relevant work easily—especially on your phone?
  3. Which direction makes you more likely to bring NOW a brief, and why?

Keep this about the experience, not a vote on every sentence. A specific sticking point or missing story is more useful than a long wish list.
