WHAT CHANGED

A clearer route from interest to enquiry.

  • A sharper introduction for regional marketing teams, plus useful answers before starting a brief.
  • More enquiry prompts, including a mobile shortcut on longer pages.
  • Full oval controls and clearer selection context, with consistent case navigation.
  • Lighter responsive photographs, including Wilson’s latest Palo Alto selection and the recovered McLaren images.
  • Distinct page descriptions, sharing previews and case breadcrumbs, prepared for search after launch approval.
  • The game retains its controls guide and persistent Back to NOW link.
