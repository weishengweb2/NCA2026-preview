Let the product set the tempo.

McLarenAsia PacificLaunch + live storytelling

The Artura Spider’s engine became part of a live musical performance.

01 / THE BRIEF

Start with the audience.

Give McLaren’s regional retailers and partners a memorable encounter with the Artura Spider at its Asian launch and dealer conference.

02 / THE KINDA DIFFERENT MOVE

Give the idea a different way in.

Record the engine and weave its sound into a live musical performance, supported by vehicle visuals on large screens. Even the entrance changed: hotel doors were removed to bring the car into the foyer.

03 / WHAT WE CONNECTED
  • Creative concept
  • Launch production
  • Music + screen content
  • Dealer experience
04 / WHAT MOVED

Carry the moment forward.

The product became the creative material for the reveal. A technical object had a physical presence, a sound and a shared moment with its audience.
